1. Uranium demand increases (Layer 1)
2. Fuel cycle becomes critical (Layer 2)
3. Buildout creates physical bottleneck (Layer 3)


The bottleneck is **fuel**, not reactors. LEU is the critical node.

## The Key Insight

**The bottleneck is fuel, not reactors.**

Everyone wants to build SMRs. But SMRs need fuel. And fuel is scarce.

LEU is the only US HALEU producer. That's the chokepoint.

If advanced reactors proliferate, LEU's $900M DOE contract becomes the template for the industry.
