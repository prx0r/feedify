## What They Do

Vertically integrated, AI-native health and pharmacy platform. Four pillars:

1. **Automated NHS Pharmacy** — Two GPhC-registered robotic dispensing hubs (Swaffham + Runcorn). NHS Distance Selling Pharmacy licences (no longer issued to new applicants). Annualised run rate >£5.5M.

2. **New Health GLP-1 Clinic** — Consumer weight-loss at new.co.uk. Direct supply from Eli Lilly (Mounjaro) AND Novo Nordisk (Wegovy). Revenue: £1.83M in August 2026 (first full month), up 809% on July. 14,487 orders.

3. **B2B Care Home Supply** — Live contract with Care UK (200+ care homes). Acquired eMARx (electronic medication administration records) for £380K.

4. **MedPal Health OS** — Aggregates 100+ wearables, runs AI triage on Google Vertex AI, routes to care, fills prescriptions via own robots.
