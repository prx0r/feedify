# SUSS MicroTec (SMHN.DE) Reality Check: Can AI Do What SUSS Does?

## The Short Answer

**No. SUSS makes coating/bonding equipment. AI can't build coating machines.**

## What SUSS Does

SUSS makes coating and bonding equipment for advanced packaging. Their equipment is used for:
- Temporary bonding
- Debonding
- Coating
- Wafer handling

## Why AI Can't Replace This

1. **Physical equipment** — You can't generate coating machines
2. **Process expertise** — Coating chemistry is empirical
3. **Customer calibration** — Each fab has unique requirements
4. **Safety certification** — Equipment must meet SEMI standards

## The Moat

SUSS's moat is PHYSICAL:
- Specialized coating equipment
- Process expertise
- €115M coater order from Taiwanese OSAT
- Customer lock-in

## The Risk

1. **Revenue lagging orders** — Backlog strong but execution needed
2. **European listing** — Limits US investor access
3. **FX risk**

## Verdict

**AI cannot do what SUSS does.** Physical equipment manufacturing.

**Confidence: 90%**
