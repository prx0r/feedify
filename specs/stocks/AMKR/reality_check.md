**No. Amkor does advanced packaging assembly. AI can't assemble chips.**

Amkor provides advanced packaging services:
- Flip-chip bonding
- Wafer-level packaging
- 2.5D/3D integration

## Why AI Can't Replace This

2. **Cleanroom operations** — Specialized manufacturing environment
3. **Equipment calibration** — Bonding machines need precise setup
4. **Yield optimization** — Process expertise
