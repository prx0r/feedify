## The Thesis

HBM4 stacks are getting:
- Thinner (die-to-die spacing shrinking)
- Taller (more dies per stack)
- Hotter (more power density)
- Closer together (finer pitch)

TOWA's molding/encapsulation equipment fills the gaps between dies. This is a physical bottleneck that can't be automated away.
