# SVCO Peer Review Gap Analysis

## What I Missed

### 1. Pipeline ≠ Revenue
**My claim:** Pipeline $292M > market cap $228M
**Reality:** RPO (contracted revenue) is only $45M, and it DECLINED from $47.1M in Dec 2025.

**Why I missed it:** I treated "pipeline" as equivalent to "backlog." SEC filings distinguish:
- Identified opportunity pipeline (unqualified leads)
- Backlog (contracted but not yet recognized)
- RPO (remaining performance obligations — actual contracted revenue)

### 2. Revenue Growth Misleading
**My claim:** Revenue +48% validates FTCO
**Reality:** TCAD revenue was only +16%. SIP (IP licensing) drove most growth.

**Why I missed it:** I looked at consolidated revenue, not segment-level. The thesis is about TCAD/FTCO, not IP licensing.

### 3. TCAD Shrank in 2025
**My claim:** Massive growth business
**Reality:** TCAD revenue fell $10.2M in FY2025.

**Why I missed it:** I only looked at recent quarters, not the full year trend.

### 4. Acquisitions Doing Heavy Lifting
**My claim:** Organic inflection
**Reality:** Three acquisitions (Cadence OPC, Tech-X, Mixel) inflated growth comparisons.

**Why I missed it:** I didn't separate organic vs inorganic growth.

### 5. Data Moat Uncertain
**My claim:** Proprietary fab calibration data moat
**Reality:** Customer owns fab data; Silvaco provides software/workflow.

**Why I missed it:** I assumed Silvaco accumulated cross-fab data. Micron's description suggests customers supply their own data.

### 6. NVIDIA Evidence Overstated
**My claim:** NVIDIA is using Silvaco
**Reality:** NVIDIA is building the enabling layer; Cadence/Synopsys are primary partners.

**Why I missed it:** I conflated "NVIDIA invested in Silvaco" with "NVIDIA uses Silvaco."

### 7. Cadence/Synopsys Competition
**My claim:** SVCO has unique position
**Reality:** Cadence + NVIDIA are doing exactly what SVCO claims (physics simulation, digital twins, AI agents).

**Why I missed it:** I didn't compare SVCO's product to what competitors are announcing.

### 8. Cash/Dilution Understated
**My claim:** $13M cash adequate
**Reality:** $35M ATM opened, already issued $11.1M in shares.

**Why I missed it:** I didn't read the SEC filing carefully enough.

### 9. Governance Risk Ignored
**My claim:** 58% insider ownership = alignment
**Reality:** Family control + litigation history + controlled company exemptions.

**Why I missed it:** I treated insider ownership as purely bullish without considering governance implications.

### 10. Book-to-Bill < 1
**My claim:** Pipeline justifies valuation
**Reality:** Bookings < revenue for two consecutive quarters.

**Why I missed it:** I didn't check bookings vs revenue trend.

## What This Teaches About Our Process

### Systematic Errors

1. **Pipeline/Backlog/Revenue confusion** — Always ask: "What is the actual contracted revenue number?"
2. **Segment vs consolidated** — Always check segment-level growth, not just total
3. **Organic vs inorganic** — Always separate acquisition effects
4. **Category vs company** — "AI needs X" doesn't mean "Company Y captures X"
5. **Customer validation vs endorsement** — "NVIDIA invested" ≠ "NVIDIA uses exclusively"

### What We Should Add to Feedify

1. **RPO tracking** — Not just pipeline/backlog
2. **Segment-level growth** — Consolidated can mislead
3. **Organic vs inorganic adjustment** — Remove acquisition effects
4. **Book-to-bill ratio** — Bookings vs revenue trend
5. **Competitor comparison** — What are SNPS/CDNS/Siemens doing?
6. **Governance risk score** — Litigation, controlled company, ATM
