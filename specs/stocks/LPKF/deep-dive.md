# LPKF Deep Dive: The Financial Case

## The Setup

LPKF is a ~650-person German laser-equipment company. At roughly €13.45/share, €335M market cap and €342M EV, the market is already assigning roughly €180–230M of enterprise value to future LIDE/advanced-packaging success.

## What LPKF Actually Is

Four businesses: Electronics, Development, Welding, Solar.

LIDE sits inside Electronics — LPKF does NOT disclose LIDE revenue separately.

LIDE (Laser Induced Deep Etching): laser modifies microscopic regions inside glass → selective chemical etching → very precise structures without cracking/heat damage.

Packaged into NEXAR family for: through-glass vias, cavities, glass interposers, glass cores, RDL ablation, glass singulation, multilayer glass bonding, eventually optical structures/CPO.

Claims production systems handle 515×510mm panels, micron-scale features, high aspect ratios.

Revenue €105-120M, EBIT margin -3% to +4.5%. H1 was €36.5M, so H2 needs €68.5-83.5M.

## The 2019 Forecast Miss

LPKF forecast >€360M revenue and ≥25% EBIT margin in 2024. Actual: €122.9M revenue, ~0% EBIT margin. Revenue reached only ~34% of projection.

## LIDE Evidence

- Dec 2024: Display/semiconductor customer ordered several systems, mid-seven-figure euro
- Q1 2026: First capacity-expansion order

## What Would Make Me Buy Aggressively

1. Semiconductor HVM ramp order (10+ systems, high-single-digit €M)
2. Second independent HVM customer
3. Electronics segment inflection (€50M+ revenue)
4. Backlog >€60-80M
5. FCF turns positive without equity issuance

## What Would Kill The Thesis

- No HVM semiconductor LIDE order by end-2027
- Glass substrate roadmaps slip beyond 2029
- Competing TGV processes bypass LPKF
- Electronics revenue stays ~€30-40M
- Another >€10M annual cash burn in 2027
- Covenant renegotiation or meaningful equity raise

## Key Metrics to Watch

**LIDE HVM orders → Electronics backlog/revenue → cash**

If those three inflect together, €335M EV becomes hard to justify.
