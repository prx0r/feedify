# Acceleration Thesis — Dev Plan

**Date:** 2026-09-08
**Status:** IMPLEMENTING

## Core Thesis

> AI reduces the price of cognition. This expands search across mathematics, software, engineering and science. Cheap verification allows machine search to become reliable. Automated laboratories connect digital intelligence to physical reality and generate proprietary training data. Better discoveries improve chips, materials, energy and computation, which lower the price of intelligence further.

## The Formula

```
R&D velocity ≈ (intelligence × parallel search × verifier quality × experimental throughput) / feedback latency
```

## Bottleneck Migration Law

1. Idea generation stops being scarce
2. Verification becomes scarce → Axiom, Harmonic, Lean
3. Experimentation becomes scarce → Periodic, Lila, Diffuse
4. Physical throughput becomes scarce → Orbital, fabs
5. Scale-up/manufacturing becomes scarce → supply chains
6. Energy/cooling/interconnect becomes scarce → Proxima, Lightmatter
7. Intelligence gets cheaper again → loop restarts
