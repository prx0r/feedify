# Quantitative Buy Recommendations — September 9, 2026

## Methodology

For each stock, I score:
1. **Thesis fit** (0-10): How well does it fit the bottleneck migration thesis?
2. **Evidence quality** (0-10): How strong is the evidence (orders, revenue, margins)?
3. **Valuation** (0-10): How cheap is it relative to thesis potential?
4. **Convexity** (0-10): How much upside if thesis works?
5. **Probability** (0-10): How likely is thesis to work?

**Composite score** = Thesis × Evidence × Valuation × Convexity × Probability / 100
