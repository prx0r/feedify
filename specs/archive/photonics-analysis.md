### The Key Insight

**The strongest thesis fits are in testing/inspection, not in photonics manufacturing.**

AEHR, FORM, KEYS, ONTO, VIAV all benefit from:
- More complex packages → more testing needed
- HBM + 2.5D + SiPh → more failure modes
- Higher value per package → cost of late defect discovery explodes
