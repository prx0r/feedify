## LPKF (LPKF.DE) — The Technology

**Price:** €13.45 | **Market Cap:** €335M

### What It Does

LPKF makes laser equipment for glass packaging. LIDE (Laser Induced Deep Etching) enables:
- Through-glass vias
- Glass interposers
- Glass cores
- Optical structures

### The Evidence

- **80% customer selection rate** — when customers compare LIDE to alternatives, 80% choose LPKF
- **Production orders** — not just development systems
- **€1.7B TAM** — glass substrates by 2030
- **€335M market cap** — 0.2x TAM
- **80% IP protection** — deep moat

### The Financials

- Revenue: €122.9M (2024)
- EBIT: -€2.5M (2024)
- Cash: €4.0M
- Equity: €63.9M
- Syndicated facility through Dec 2028

### The DCF

- Terminal EBIT multiple: 15×
- Discount rate: 14%
- Base value: ~€20-21/share
- Range: €15-25

### The Scenarios

| Scenario | 2030 Revenue | EBIT | Fair Value |
|----------|-------------|------|------------|
| Bear | €140M | €12-15M | €4-8/share |
| Base | €260-280M | €50-55M | €18-25/share |

### The Risk

- Management's 2019 forecast miss (34% of projection)

**Verdict: High-risk, high-reward. The technology is real. The orders need to come.**
