### Why This Matters

The current semiconductor design flow is:
1. Human designs chip (slow, expensive)
2. Simulate with TCAD (slow, but faster than fabricating)
3. Fabricate (very slow, very expensive)
4. Test (slow, expensive)
5. If it doesn't work, go back to step 1

AI makes step 1 faster. But steps 2-5 are still physics-constrained.

**Silvaco owns step 2.**

### The Key Insight

AI agents are **automating the use** of TCAD tools, not replacing them. The chain is:
```
AI agent generates design → TCAD simulates → AI interprets → AI refines
```

Silvaco IS the TCAD simulation layer. AI creates MORE demand for it.
