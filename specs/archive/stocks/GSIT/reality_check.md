# GSI Technology (GSIT) Reality Check: Can AI Do What GSIT Does?

## The Short Answer

**Partially. AI can design compute-in-memory architectures, but GSIT builds the physical hardware.**

## What GSIT Does

GSIT makes SRAM and is developing compute-in-memory (CIM) processors. Their Plato architecture uses:
- Associative processing
- In-memory computing
- Data movement elimination

## Why AI Can't Fully Replace This

1. **Physical SRAM manufacturing** — You can't generate SRAM chips
2. **CIM architecture expertise** — Specialized hardware design
3. **Customer-specific optimization** — Each workload needs tuning
4. **Manufacturing relationships** — Foundry access

## The Moat

GSIT's moat is PHYSICAL + ARCHITECTURAL:
- SRAM manufacturing capability
- CIM architecture IP
- Plato tapeout scheduled March 2027
- Customer relationships

## The Risk

1. **Revenue flat** — Only +0.5% growth
2. **Binary outcome** — Plato works or doesn't
3. **History of aggressive promises**
4. **Dilution risk**

## Verdict

**AI can help design CIM architectures, but GSIT builds the physical hardware.**

**Confidence: 70%** (binary outcome)
