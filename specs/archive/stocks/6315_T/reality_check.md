# TOWA (6315.T) Reality Check: Can AI Do What TOWA Does?

## The Short Answer

**No. TOWA makes physical manufacturing equipment. AI can't build machines.**

## What TOWA Does

TOWA makes molding/encapsulation equipment for HBM stacks. As HBM stacks get:
- Thinner
- Taller
- Hotter
- Closer together

Molding becomes critical. TOWA's equipment is what fills the gaps between dies.

## Why AI Can't Replace This

1. **Physical manufacturing equipment** — You can't 3D print a molding machine with an AI
2. **Customer-specific calibration** — Each HBM stack has unique geometry
3. **30+ years of process knowledge** — Encapsulation chemistry is empirical
4. **Supply chain relationships** — SK Hynix, Micron, Samsung trust TOWA

## The Moat

TOWA's moat is PHYSICAL, not digital:
- Specialized machinery that takes years to develop
- Customer-specific calibration data
- Process expertise accumulated over decades
- Supply chain lock-in

## The Risk

1. **Hybrid bonding could replace molding** — If bonding technology advances, molding becomes less critical
2. **Japanese listing** — Limits Western investor access
3. **Revenue lagging orders** — Backlog is strong but execution needed

## Verdict

**AI cannot do what TOWA does.** This is a pure physical manufacturing play.

**Confidence: 95%**
