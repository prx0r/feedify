# ASYS (Amtech) Reality Check: Can AI Do What Amtech Does?

## The Short Answer

**No. Amtech makes thermal processing equipment. AI can't build furnaces.**

## What Amtech Does

Amtech makes thermal processing equipment for semiconductor packaging. Their TPS segment makes:
- Diffusion furnaces
- Oxidation systems
- Annealing systems

AI applications are >40% of TPS revenue.

## Why AI Can't Replace This

1. **Physical equipment** — You can't generate a furnace with software
2. **Process knowledge** — Thermal profiles are empirical
3. **Customer calibration** — Each fab has unique requirements
4. **Safety certification** — Equipment must meet SEMI standards

## The Moat

Amtech's moat is PHYSICAL:
- Specialized thermal equipment
- Process expertise
- Safety certifications
- Customer relationships

## The Risk

1. **Dilution** — Recent $56.5M equity raise
2. **Execution** — AI revenue ramp needs to materialize
3. **Small cap** — High volatility

## Verdict

**AI cannot do what Amtech does.** Physical equipment manufacturing.

**Confidence: 90%**
