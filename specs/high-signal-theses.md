## Thesis 1: AI Co-Design Is Replacing Sequential Hardware Design

**Statement:** The industry is moving from "design chip, then optimize for workload" to "jointly optimize chip and workload simultaneously."

**Supporting Papers:**
- **Redwood** (arxiv:2608.26418) — Architect Labs built an AI accelerator end-to-end by AI in 2 weeks. "The system autonomously generated the performance model, RTL design, UVM environments, formal proofs, firmware, and kernels."
- **A3C3** (arxiv:2606.20869) — "Jointly optimizes neural network architectures and their hardware implementations... avoids the sub-optimality of traditional top-down AI system design."
- **CHIA** (arxiv:2606.27350) — Open-source framework for "agentic AI-driven hardware and software design flows" with 5 case studies including RTL generation.
- **Beacon** (arxiv:2608.30932) — "LLM Multi-Agent Driven Hardware Design Space Exploration... reduces composite latency-energy-monetary-cost by 25.1%–93.5%."

**Supporting Feedify Data:**
- @axi_master (Architect Labs): "We had to build something similar for our AI systems to autonomously improve firmware and kernels"
- @ahsu___ (Architect Labs): "Introducing Project Redwood" — first AI-designed chip
- @KyriectionZhang (xAI): "Grok 4.6 is live. We've been working hard to make Grok better at automating its own infrastructure."

**Falsification:** If Architect's tapeout results don't match claims, or if co-design fails to improve PPA vs sequential design.

## Thesis 2: Formal Verification Is the Bridge to Trustworthy AI-Designed Systems

**Statement:** AI can generate code/chips, but formal verification is what makes them trustworthy. The bottleneck is verification, not generation.

**Supporting Papers:**
- **Forge** (arxiv:2606.22413) — "Pipeline produces standards-relevant verification evidence for LLM-generated Java... every run converges to fully verified code."
- **Broken by Default** (arxiv:2604.05292) — "55.8% of AI-generated artifacts contain vulnerabilities... Formal verification via Z3 SMT solving is the only approach that can establish ground-truth exploitability."
- **NL2VC** (arxiv:2604.22601) — "Gemma 4-31B achieved 90.91% verification success rate... formal verification is now attainable for open-weight LLMs."
- **Neuro-Formal Verification** (arxiv:2608.21516) — "AI coding agent translates, established verifier decides... machine-checked proof."

**Supporting Feedify Data:**
- @llllvvuu (Harmonic): Posts about Lean, Mathlib failure modes, formalization details
- @tachim (Harmonic): "Formal-verifiable mathematical reasoning"
- @axi_master (Architect): "Formal proofs" as part of Redwood's pipeline

**Falsification:** If formal verification can't scale to complex real-world systems, or if AI-generated proofs are unreliable.

## The Meta-Thesis

All 10 theses support one overarching claim:

> **The rate of technological progress is itself increasing, and the bottleneck continuously migrates from cognition → verification → experimentation → manufacturing → energy. The companies that solve each bottleneck in sequence will capture disproportionate value.**

This is testable:
- Track when labs report "verification is our bottleneck"
- Track when labs report "experimentation is our bottleneck"
- Track when manufacturing constraints become binding
- Track when energy/interconnect becomes the limiting factor

Feedify is the sensor array for this.
