## What We Hoped to Get

- Failure modes of autonomous researchers
- Evidence of bottleneck migration inside training
- How self-improvement actually works in practice
- Whether AI can design AI better than humans

## What We Got

### @braaannigan (1 August tweet)
**Key finding**: "my autonomous researcher keeps getting trapped in local search"
- Models jump to hyperparameter optimization too early
- Converge to local optima
- Remain below what good human researchers achieve
- **Alpha**: Where the actual bottleneck in recursive science is — not LLM knowledge, but research-search strategy / scientific taste

### @GenAI_is_real (24 August tweets)
**Key finding**: "Barrel Effect" — everyone optimizes train/inference alignment (mathematically elegant, publishable) while the actual limiting stave migrated to sandbox reliability, reward pipelines, observability, external-environment throughput
- "The bottleneck moved somewhere else a long time ago"
- **Alpha**: Our entire investment ontology applied inside frontier AI engineering

### @jxmnop (17 August tweets)
**Key finding**: Research-level thinking around RL/self-distillation/forgetting
- How knowledge retention works in continual learning
- **Alpha**: Technical details on how self-improvement actually fails

## Top Alpha from This Category

1. **Bottleneck migration is real inside AI training** — @GenAI_is_real's Barrel Effect is the clearest statement of our thesis from inside frontier AI
2. **Autonomous researchers fail at search strategy** — @braaannigan shows the actual bottleneck is scientific taste, not knowledge
3. **Memory systems are broken** — @itzik009 argues current "memory" stores context without changing behavior
4. **Self-improvement requires new data paradigms** — @YisiSang's A-Evolve shows AI can post-train itself but needs agentic data
