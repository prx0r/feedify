## What We Got

### @CanAztekin (0 August — last tweet Sep 7)
**Key finding**: Species-specific oxygen sensing gates limb regeneration
- Published in Science (April 2026)
- Lowering oxygen in mouse limbs induces regenerative programs
- Tadpoles/axolotls less sensitive to O2 than mice
- **Alpha**: One low-level physical constraint (oxygen sensing) gates a huge biological capability. This is the kind of mechanistic surprise that changes investment theses.

### @msoufi_bioe (0 August — last tweet Jul 22)
**Key finding**: Building synthetic cells from the bottom up
- "Engineering biology needs to be more routinized"
- Cell-free ribosomes as programmable primitives
- **Alpha**: The gap between "we can edit genes" and "we can build cells from scratch" is where the investment opportunity lies

### @mmslabodnick (0 August — last tweet Nov 2024)
**Key finding**: Stentor (single-celled organism) solves shape-control problems
- Cells compute morphological decisions
- **Alpha**: Bioelectricity as a computational substrate — cells make decisions without neurons
